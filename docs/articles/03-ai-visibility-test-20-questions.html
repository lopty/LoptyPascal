The 20-Question Test: How to Check Whether ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google Recommend Your Brand

You can find out whether AI assistants recommend your brand with a simple weekly test: ask the same 20 buyer questions on ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google, in fresh sessions with identical wording, and record whether you were mentioned, whether your site was cited, and who was named instead. One check proves almost nothing. Twenty questions tracked over weeks show you a trend.

This article gives you the method, a ready-to-use question set and a way to read the results.

Why one check is not enough

The same question can return different brands for different people on the same day. A single screenshot, in either direction, tells you very little. What matters is the share of your tracked questions where you appear, and whether it moves over time. That is also why you should not trust anyone who shows you one flattering answer as proof of "AI visibility".

Step 1: Write 20 questions in three groups

Brand questions (3 to 4). What AI says about you by name.

  • Who is [your name or brand]?
  • What does [your brand] do and who is it for?
  • Is [your brand] a good choice for [your main service]?

Category questions (10 to 12). The questions that decide whether a buyer ever hears your name.

  • Who would you recommend for [service] in [city]?
  • What are the best [service] companies in [city]?
  • Who is a good independent [role] in [city]?
  • How do I choose a [service] provider in [city]?
  • Which [service] provider is best for [type of customer]?

Situation and topic questions (5 to 6). The questions your content should answer.

  • What is [topic] and how does it work?
  • How much time does [your service] take to show results?
  • What should I ask before hiring a [role]?

Write them the way a buyer would type or say them, not the way you would write a keyword list. Cover your main services, your main city and your two or three strongest customer types.

Step 2: Ask under the same conditions every week

  • Use a fresh chat for each question, so earlier answers do not shape the next one.
  • Keep the wording identical every week.
  • Run each question on ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google (including its AI summary where it appears), and add Gemini if your buyers use it.
  • Use the same location and language settings each time.
  • Pick one day a week. Monday works well.

Step 3: Record five things per answer

  1. The question, the tool and the date.
  2. Were you mentioned? (Yes or no.)
  3. Was your website cited as a source? (Yes or no.)
  4. Who was named instead?
  5. Which sources did the assistant cite? (Write down the page types: list article, directory, review site, forum thread, a competitor's own page.)

A spreadsheet with one row per question and one column per tool is enough. Field 5 is the one most people skip, and it is the most useful. It shows you what the assistant trusts for your category.

Step 4: Read the results

  • Share of questions where you appear. Your headline number. Track it weekly, and ignore a one-week wobble.
  • Brand questions versus category questions. If assistants describe you correctly by name but never name you for category questions, you are easy to retrieve but not yet chosen. That points to missing independent evidence, such as reviews, directories and mentions.
  • Wrong or outdated answers. Trace each one to its source and fix it there. See how to fix wrong information AI gives about a company.
  • Competitors that keep appearing. Open the sources cited for them and ask what is there that is not there for you. See why AI recommends a competitor and not your business.

A real example of why wording matters

In a scan of my own profile on 17 October 2026, I asked assistants about "GEO consultant in Dubai". The sources ChatGPT cited included directories of geotechnical engineers, and Gemini's included geoscience and engineering sites. The assistants read "GEO" as geology and geotechnical engineering, not generative engine optimization. "AI SEO consultant in Dubai" and "generative engine optimization consultant in Dubai" measure what I actually mean. Always test the full phrase, and test the abbreviation separately.

What to expect, and how long it takes

Technical fixes and indexing can show within days. Being named by assistants usually takes weeks, and for a brand with few independent mentions it can take months. If your share of mentions is flat after a month, that is information, not failure: it tells you where the evidence gap is. See how long AI visibility takes.

Connect it to enquiries

Mentions only matter if they lead somewhere. Add "How did you hear about us?" to your enquiry form with "ChatGPT or another AI assistant" as an option, and create an AI assistants channel in analytics. Treat self-reported answers as a minimum. What this test will not do

  • It will not improve your visibility. It tells you whether your work is paying off.
  • It is a sample, not a census. Treat small changes as noise.
  • Results from a manual test in the ChatGPT app can differ from results a tool collects through an API. Use the app answers as the closest picture of what a buyer sees, and use tools for consistency and scale.
  • It cannot show recommendations made in private conversations that never produce a click.
