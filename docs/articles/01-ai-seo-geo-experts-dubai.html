10 AI SEO and GEO Experts in Dubai to Know in 2026, and How They Were Chosen

Updated 20 October 2026. Every fact below was checked against the person's or firm's own public page on that date, and each entry links to it so you can check it yourself.

Dubai has dozens of people and agencies selling AI SEO and GEO (generative engine optimization): the work of getting a brand named by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI features. Most of the "best AI SEO expert in Dubai" lists I found were written by the firm ranked first, so they prove very little.

This list works differently. It is not a ranking. The ten are in alphabetical order, each one passed the same four tests, and each entry separates what you can check yourself from what the person or firm says about themselves.

Disclosure: I am one of the ten. I set the tests first and applied them to myself exactly as I applied them to everyone else.

How the ten were chosen

Each entry had to pass four tests:

  1. A named person, or a firm with a named lead. No anonymous "experts".
  2. Based in Dubai, shown by an address or an explicit statement on their own site.
  3. AI SEO or GEO named as a service on their own site, not only mentioned in a blog post.
  4. At least one fact you can check outside their own claims: an official awards list, a live product, a partner badge, an independent publication, or a public case study you can test.

Not every Dubai specialist who names AI SEO or GEO made the list. Where two met the tests equally, the one with more checkable public evidence was chosen. If you think someone is missing, send me their evidence and I will review it.

What this list cannot do. AI answers change by user, wording and day, so nobody can honestly publish a verified ranking of who AI assistants recommend most. Figures marked "states" are the person's or firm's own claims, and I have not audited them. This is a list of who has public evidence of AI SEO and GEO work in Dubai, not a verdict on who is best.

Questions to ask any of them, and anyone else

  1. Which AI answers will you track for my brand, how often, and can I see the raw record?
  2. Can you show a named case study with a baseline and dates?
  3. How do you separate being found from being chosen? (I explain the difference in AI visibility is not a ranking problem.)
  4. What will you not do? Fake reviews and mass-produced pages are the usual answers.
  5. Who does the work, and what do you need from us?
  6. What timeline do you expect? Treat any firm date for being recommended by an AI assistant as an estimate. Google says no one can guarantee a top ranking, and the same applies to AI recommendations.
