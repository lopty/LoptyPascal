# How to Increase Your Brand's AI Visibility and Get Recommended by AI Assistants: 9 Steps

To get recommended by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI features, make your brand easy to find, easy to verify and easy to choose. In practice that means five things: a site AI crawlers can read, one consistent description of who you are, pages that answer buyer questions directly, independent sources that say the same about you, and a way to measure what changes.

No one can force an AI assistant to recommend a business, and results usually take weeks to months. But every step below is in your control. Each one links to a longer guide on [loptypascal.com](https://loptypascal.com/insights).

**Why this matters now.** Pew Research Center tracked the browsing of 900 U.S. adults in March 2025. When Google showed an AI summary, users clicked a traditional search result in 8% of visits, compared with 15% when no summary appeared, and clicked a link inside the AI summary in just 1% of visits ([Pew Research Center, July 2025](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/)). It is one month of U.S. data on Google, but the direction is clear: more decisions are shaped before anyone visits your website.

## 1. Find out what AI says about you today

You cannot improve what you have not measured. Write 15 to 20 questions a real buyer would ask, from narrow ("who offers X in Dubai Marina") to broad ("how do I choose an X"). Ask them each week on ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google, in a fresh session, with identical wording. Record whether you were mentioned, whether your site was cited, and who was named instead.

## 2. Make sure AI crawlers can read your site

Google can render JavaScript. Most AI crawlers do not. A site that looks perfect in a browser can send crawlers an almost empty page. Check it in a minute: open "View page source" on a key page and search for a sentence you can see on screen. Then check your robots.txt allows the crawlers you want, such as GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot.

Learn more: [why crawlers see an empty page on your website](https://loptypascal.com/insights/why-crawlers-see-an-empty-page) and [should you allow GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot](https://loptypascal.com/insights/ai-crawlers-robots-txt).

## 3. State who you are in one sentence, and use it everywhere

An assistant has to know which business or person it is reading about. Write one positioning line and one short bio, and use them unchanged on your site, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile and every directory. Correct or remove old listings that describe you differently. If an assistant already says something wrong about you, trace the source and fix it there.

Learn more: [what entity SEO is and why AI gets your brand wrong](https://loptypascal.com/insights/what-is-entity-seo) and [how to fix wrong information AI gives about a company](https://loptypascal.com/insights/ai-gives-wrong-information-about-my-company).

## 4. Answer buyer questions first

Put the direct answer in the first two or three sentences of each page, then explain it with specifics: steps, conditions, sourced numbers, and what does not apply. Write one page per question. The 2023 research paper that named GEO found that its top-performing methods, citing sources, adding quotations and adding statistics, improved visibility in generated answers by 30 to 40% on its main metric, while keyword stuffing offered little to no improvement ([Aggarwal et al., arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735)). It is one benchmark, so treat it as a direction, not a law.

Learn more: [how to write a page so AI can cite it](https://loptypascal.com/insights/how-to-write-content-ai-can-cite).

## 5. Add structured data that matches the page

Structured data (schema.org markup) removes ambiguity: this page is about this organisation, this person, this service. Mark up only what a visitor can see. Google says its AI features need no special markup, so treat it as clarity, not a switch. The same goes for llms.txt: cheap and harmless, but not confirmed as an input to any major assistant's recommendations.

Learn more: [does structured data affect AI answers](https://loptypascal.com/insights/structured-data-for-ai-answers) and [does llms.txt help you appear in AI answers](https://loptypascal.com/insights/does-llms-txt-work).

## 6. Earn evidence from outside your own website

This is the step most brands skip. A perfectly optimized website can make you easier to find while doing little to make you the one chosen, if nearly every claim about you comes from you. Assistants can draw on reviews, directories, publications, databases, comparison pages and community discussions. Collect real reviews on independent platforms, ask clients for specific written recommendations, publish expert answers where buyers ask questions, and seek genuine mentions in publications and podcasts. Never buy or write reviews.

Learn more: [AI visibility is not a ranking problem](https://loptypascal.com/insights/pitchxpo-launchpad-2026-ai-visibility), [do reviews affect whether AI recommends a business](https://loptypascal.com/insights/do-reviews-affect-ai-recommendations) and [how a business gets recommended by ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google AI Overviews](https://loptypascal.com/insights/how-to-get-recommended-by-chatgpt).

## 7. Study who AI recommends instead of you

Assistants often show their sources. Ask your tracked questions, open every cited page, and note what kind it is: a list article, a directory, a review site, a forum thread, a competitor's own page. That tells you what the assistants trust for your category. Then close the gap with real work, not tricks.

Learn more: [why AI recommends a competitor and not your business](https://loptypascal.com/insights/competitor-recommended-by-ai) and [why you can rank on Google but not be mentioned by ChatGPT](https://loptypascal.com/insights/ranking-on-google-but-not-in-chatgpt).

## 8. Count the enquiries AI sends you

Add "How did you hear about us?" to every enquiry form with "ChatGPT or another AI assistant" as an option, ask the same on calls and WhatsApp, and build an AI assistants channel in analytics from referrers such as chatgpt.com and perplexity.ai. Treat self-reported answers as a minimum, because many people forget where they first heard of you.

Learn more: [how to track traffic and leads from ChatGPT and Perplexity](https://loptypascal.com/insights/tracking-leads-from-chatgpt-and-perplexity).

## 9. Keep your SEO foundations, and be patient

Google says its AI features rely on the same foundations as Search: a page must be indexed and eligible to show in Search, and no special optimization is needed beyond normal good practice ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)). So do not drop SEO. Technical fixes and indexing can show within days. Being recommended usually takes weeks, and for a brand with few independent mentions it can take months.

## What this will not do

- It will not make an AI assistant recommend you. Answers vary by user, wording and day, and no one controls them.
- It will not replace a real reputation. Assistants lean on what independent sources say about you, so reviews and mentions still have to be earned.
- It will not work as a set of tricks. The methods that help are the ones that make your business more useful and easier to verify.
