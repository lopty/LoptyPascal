# Dubai search positioning review

Research date: 8 October 2026. This is a comparison of visible public material, not an audit of competitors' analytics or proof of why any particular AI system selected a business. Repeated recommendation frequency needs a fixed prompt set and saved answers to measure.

## 1. Raaquib Pathan

[Homepage](https://raaquib.com/), [Dubai GEO service](https://raaquib.com/aeo-geo-consultant-dubai), [SaaS service](https://raaquib.com/saas-seo-consultant), [identity page](https://raaquib.com/llm-info).

Visible strengths: a consistent individual consultant identity, Dubai location, focused SEO/AI search services and prominent case-study links. The homepage connects measurable outcomes with sectors and a career timeline. Separate commercial pages cover different buyer needs. His LLM information page restates identity and services; its instruction-like wording is marketing content, not independent validation or a requirement for AI systems to follow.

External corroboration exists: [Hunter credits a template to Raaquib at Salesmate](https://hunter.io/templates/sales/book-meeting/sales-team-goals), and [GoDaddy quotes his contribution](https://www.godaddy.com/resources/skills/how-to-write-an-effective-out-of-office-message). These establish a named professional footprint; they do not independently verify every portfolio metric or a best-in-Dubai title.

Inference: the combination makes it easier to resolve the person, match a specific service query and retrieve evidence. It does not establish the internal reason behind any recommendation.

## 2. AIIMS Group

[Dubai SEO page](https://aiims.group/en-ae/seo-agency-dubai/).

The page combines a Dubai office identity, named regional projects, visual evidence, a featured Gebal case study and linked services for Arabic, multilingual and AI search. Case-study metrics are presented beside the client and links to fuller project context. Awards are displayed as agency claims; award issuers were not independently audited in this review.

Inference: recognisable local business details plus named projects provide useful material for both prospective buyers and search retrieval. Its agency scale is different from an individual consultant; Lopty should emphasise personal delivery and building Prezlo rather than imitate an agency headcount.

## 3. RATH Infotech

[Homepage](https://www.rath.ae/), [case studies](https://www.rath.ae/case-studies/), [Meta Ads example](https://www.rath.ae/case-studies/real-estate-meta-ads-multi-market/).

The strongest visible pattern is extensive, organised service, industry, location and guide coverage. The case-study hub separates engagements and identifies anonymised client reporting. Its commercial pages have clear paths to related help and contact.

Inference: this gives the site many relevant retrieval entry points. Page volume alone is not proof of quality or the cause of recommendations. Lopty already has 61 existing pages; original client evidence is a better next investment than dozens of near-duplicate service pages.

## 4. ASAD

[Homepage](https://www.asad.ae/), [case studies](https://www.asad.ae/case-studies/).

ASAD leads with an explicit AI SEO agency identity and Dubai location. It connects GEO, ChatGPT SEO, local SEO and industry pages. Its homepage defines reporting measures and its case-study hub links named client outcomes. The hub says nine engagements but only two case links were visible in the retrieved page; the advertised total was not independently validated.

Inference: clear specialisation plus specific project stories offers stronger citation material than generic claims. Its published metrics remain its own reporting, not a neutral comparative ranking.

## What to apply to Lopty's site

Implemented four original case-study pages with nine supplied evidence images, distinct managed-client/founder attribution, reporting context, Article metadata, related services and visible cards. These bring the site to 65 prerendered pages. The studies are linked from home, work, services and sector pages, and included in sitemap.xml and llms.txt. The latter is an optional discovery file, not a ranking mechanism.

Carpools: start January 15, 2026; initial results within three weeks, as confirmed by Lopty. Screenshot period May–October: 797 profile interactions and 283 calls. These do not quantify a before/after uplift or ad return.

AD Residences: start August 5, 2026; results in September, confirmed by Lopty. This replaces the earlier under-one-month wording. The supplied commercial-query screenshot supports visibility; it does not show a complete comparison against Bayut or Property Finder. AI evidence has its own query/model counts.

haus & haus: 183 AI mentions and +210% displayed in the 30-day Prezlo summary. Platform scores and tracked positions are not Google rankings. Exact calendar endpoints are not visible.

Prezlo: founder project. 2.57K clicks, 303K impressions and a separate 2.18K generative AI impression total. Daily clicks cross 200 near the end of the chart, not throughout April–June. Duplicate attachments were not republished.

## Next seven days

1. Deploy the updated site and submit https://loptypascal.com/sitemap.xml. Check important URLs in Search Console for indexing and correct canonicals.
2. Keep the same person name, portrait, role and website link across the existing public profiles. Ask clients for concise, attributable testimonials or their own links to the project story when appropriate; do not manufacture independent endorsements.
3. Establish a small fixed query set covering digital marketing consultant Dubai, AI SEO expert Dubai, GEO consultant Dubai and relevant real-estate/local-business intent. Record platform, date, query, location where available, cited URLs and whether the result names Lopty.
4. Compare repeated checks on those same conditions and track qualified enquiries separately. Use missing evidence to improve existing pages before adding more general articles.

Early SEO results are possible; there is no universal three-month minimum. A top recommendation next week cannot be scheduled or promised. The practical deliverable is stronger accessible evidence, indexation checks and a repeatable visibility baseline.

Google's [current generative AI optimisation guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) prioritises useful original content and existing SEO fundamentals. It explicitly discourages unsupported shortcuts and unnecessary AI text files. This supports publishing specific project evidence rather than treating schema, repeated superlatives or an LLM-info page as a trust guarantee.
