For now, a narrative story can describe the challenge, approach, exact buyer questions and captured outcome without headline figures. Later, percentages need both the baseline and comparison values, equal reporting windows, and a named metric. A zero baseline has no meaningful percentage increase. For coverage rates, keep percentage-point change separate from relative percentage growth.

## Final story structure

1. Project context: industry, market, scope and period.
2. Plain-language summary.
3. Challenge and starting point.
4. Approach: specific work actually done.
5. Buyer questions and observed outcome.
6. Evidence: screenshots, sources, dates and measurement definitions.
7. Interpretation, limits and what happened next.
8. FAQs and relevant service/pricing links.

Public categories: `/work/ai-seo` for AI SEO, LLM and GEO stories; `/work/seo` for traditional and local search. The current site uses shared content data, so future stories can be added without rebuilding the page design.
