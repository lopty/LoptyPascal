## What to measure after deployment

- Use signed-out or fresh conversations and the same platform, location, language and wording when comparing results. Keep branded recognition separate from unbranded shortlisting.
- Record date, question, relevant context, full answer, cited URLs and whether each provider is mentioned, cited or recommended. Treat these checks as a sample.
- Check Search Console for impressions and queries on the new pages. Check analytics or Prezlo separately for AI referrals and recorded contact actions. Confirm enquiries and sales from business records.
- Recheck the same questions on a consistent schedule before adding adjacent pages. Create another page only if the new buyer decision needs a different answer or there is useful new evidence.
